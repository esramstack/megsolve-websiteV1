Three organisations, one calling platform.

Cisco Webex Calling design, migration, and unified communications assessment delivered in parallel for a county government, a state emergency agency, and an enterprise reseller.

The opportunity

01 · CONTEXT

Three organisations needed to move to Cisco Webex Calling on overlapping timelines: Gwinnett County, the Georgia Emergency Management Agency, and SHI.

The work spanned centralised management portal configuration, communication interface setup, migration strategy, PSTN connectivity, and a full unified communications audit. Each organisation carried different legacy constraints and different definitions of done.

Delivered on a time and materials Statement of Work across four months with a team of four covering project management, network engineering, security, and support.

The solution

02 · DELIVERY

Three parallel workstreams, each scoped to its own organisation but sharing a common design and testing discipline.

PH 01

Gwinnett County: Control Hub and go-live

  • Control Hub configured as the centralised management portal for the Webex Calling environment
  • User provisioning, policy configurations, and global settings established
  • Local Gateway SIP configured to bridge on-premises infrastructure and the Webex cloud
  • Thorough testing completed before transition to go-live
  • Solution deployed to 12 designated departments
  • Troubleshooting plan implemented for Webex Calling and Cisco Unified Border Element
PH 02

GEMA: migration strategy and design

  • Requirements expanded collaboratively with customer personnel
  • Detailed migration strategy documented with diagrams, charts, and configuration specifics
  • Actions, responsibilities, testing, and rollback and contingency plans defined
  • DID schemes, password schemes, and configuration details collected
  • PSTN connections configured, tested, and verified
PH 03

GEMA: deployment and cutover

  • Customer Control Hub deployed
  • SSO and Azure AD user sync configured
  • DIDs ported and auto attendant migrated
  • Training support provided to customer staff
  • Dedicated post-cutover support delivered through to customer sign-off
PH 04

SHI: UC audit and requirements gathering

  • Initial meeting facilitated to document current and future state UC requirements
  • Remote technical data extraction performed on existing telephony systems using Pivot/CC Uplinks
PH 05

SHI: analysis and recommendations

  • Discovery and extraction data analysed together
  • Future-state options identified across hardware, software, scalability, and SIP interoperability
  • Solution Assessment Document drafted and presented with executive summary
  • Current-state architecture, limitations, and redundancy configurations reviewed
  • Solution Overview Diagrams delivered for current and recommended end-state architectures

The impact

03 · OUTCOME

Gwinnett County

  • Project completed within the designated timeframe and budget
  • Webex Calling live across 12 departments following successful validation
  • Robust troubleshooting plan in place for ongoing performance

GEMA

  • Design document, system test plan, and customer approval delivered at the design gate
  • SSO and Azure AD sync, DID porting, and training support completed
  • Customer sign-off obtained following dedicated post-cutover support

SHI

  • Solution Assessment Document delivered with detailed recommendations
  • Architecture diagrams supplied for both current and recommended end states
