The opportunity

01 · CONTEXT

Greenball manufactures and wholesales industrial, agricultural, and specialty tyres from Long Beach, California, with branches across five states. Its surveillance estate had aged badly.

Five outdated bullet cameras were providing neither the coverage nor the image quality required. A dome camera in the main office was misplaced for effective monitoring. Two Hikvision NVRs were producing an inefficient and unreliable recording system. Underneath it all, the network lacked the infrastructure, including a CAT6 run from dock to MDF, to carry the data properly.

The facility needed high-definition surveillance and the network to support it, installed and configured within five working days.

The solution

02 · DELIVERY

A single mobilisation covering camera replacement, repositioning, outdoor expansion, and a network upgrade, with certified Verkada expertise on site.

PH 01

Assessment and planning

  • Comprehensive site survey conducted to evaluate existing infrastructure
  • Optimal camera placements identified against the building layout
  • Specific security and operational requirements agreed with the Greenball team
PH 02

Camera installation

  • Five outdated bullet cameras removed and five new Verkada dome cameras installed indoors
  • Existing main-office dome camera repositioned to the centre of the floor for improved coverage
  • New cameras installed at the loading dock, main gate, and in front of the main office
  • Existing CAT6 cabling reused for most outdoor cameras, cutting installation time and cost
PH 03

Network infrastructure upgrade

  • Two outdated Hikvision NVRs decommissioned and removed from the MDF
  • Two new 24-port switches installed to support the increased camera load
  • New CAT6 cable run from the dock to the MDF, closing the infrastructure gap
PH 04

Long-cable challenge

  • High-quality industry-standard cable sourced to minimise signal degradation over the long run
  • Additional cable management solutions included to mitigate length-related issues
  • Additional cost communicated transparently with supporting documentation
PH 05

Disposal, access equipment and handover

  • Old equipment and waste disposed of responsibly using on-site dumpsters
  • Scissor lift used for one day to install outdoor cameras safely and efficiently
  • Certified Verkada installation expert engaged to verify setup and configuration

The impact

03 · OUTCOME

Enhanced security coverage

  • Camera coverage increased by 30% through five new Verkada dome cameras
  • Blind spots significantly reduced across the facility

Network and operational performance

  • Network performance improved by 40% via new switches and the dedicated CAT6 run
  • 25% improvement in operational efficiency from repositioning and advanced Verkada technology
  • Outdoor camera integration on existing cabling reduced installation time by 20%

Cost and environmental discipline

  • 15% reduction in extra costs through strategic cable management
  • 90% reduction in e-waste against previous projects through proper disposal

Delivery and future-proofing

  • 100% success rate configuring Verkada cameras and integrating them into the existing system
  • Installation and configuration completed inside the five-day window
  • Security infrastructure future-proofed for evolving surveillance technology
