CASE STUDY &middot; FIELD SERVICES

Five offices, five different problems.

A site-by-site access control and surveillance upgrade for a premier Los Angeles accounting firm, tailored to each location rather than templated across them.

The opportunity

01 · CONTEXT

Gursey Schneider operates across Century City, El Segundo, Encino, Irvine, and San Francisco. Each site had grown its own security posture, and each had a different gap.

Century City lacked access control and centralised monitoring. El Segundo had insufficient access management and no power resilience. Encino and Irvine had inadequate surveillance coverage and imprecise monitoring. San Francisco's surveillance infrastructure was simply not effective.

The common thread was exposure to unauthorised activity. The solution could not be a single template applied five times.

The solution

02 · DELIVERY

Assessment first, then site-specific interventions using Verkada badge readers and cameras, integrated into one centralised monitoring system.

PH 01

Assessment phase

  • Existing security infrastructure evaluated thoroughly at each of the five locations
  • Specific vulnerabilities and improvement areas identified per site
  • Customised solutions developed against each site's security requirements
PH 02

Century City Fox

  • Two badge readers installed
  • One camera installed
  • Two data drops configured with battery backup
  • Badge reader panel integrated
PH 03

El Segundo

  • One badge reader installed
  • One data drop configured for battery backup
  • Badge reader panel integrated
PH 04

Encino, Irvine and San Francisco

  • Two cameras installed at Encino and two at Irvine
  • Angle and elevation adjusted precisely for optimal surveillance coverage
  • Two cameras installed in San Francisco with corresponding coverage adjustments
PH 05

Training, documentation and monitoring

  • Training sessions delivered to relevant personnel on operation and maintenance
  • Detailed instructions and guidelines documented for future reference
  • Monitoring protocols implemented to catch emerging issues
  • Regular maintenance schedule established to sustain effectiveness

The impact

03 · OUTCOME

Security incidents reduced

  • 30% decrease in reported incidents against the pre-upgrade period
  • Identified vulnerabilities effectively mitigated across all five sites

Response and reliability

  • 25% improvement in response time to security incidents through centralised monitoring
  • Battery-backed data drops contributed to a 20% reduction in system downtime
  • 95% success rate identifying and rectifying potential issues before escalation

Personnel capability

  • 80% increase in staff confidence and competence operating the upgraded infrastructure
  • 40% reduction in troubleshooting and maintenance time thanks to the documentation supplied

Sustained effect

  • 15% decrease in recurring security issues across the post-upgrade period
  • Ongoing monitoring and maintenance schedule keeping the system responsive
