CASE STUDY &middot; SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT

Suppliers, orders and payments in one place.

A centralised vendor management application replacing spreadsheet tracking across the clinic's supplier base.

Client
Dr. Salman Aesthetics
Engagement
Software Development
Timeline
3 months
Scope
Vendor Operations

The opportunity

01 · CONTEXT

Vendor tracking ran on spreadsheets. The consequences were predictable and recurring: missed payments, duplicate orders, and no visibility into which suppliers were actually performing.

With no single source of truth, procurement decisions were made on memory rather than record.

The solution

02 · DELIVERY

A centralised application covering the full vendor lifecycle from supplier record to payment reconciliation.

PH 01

Supplier and order management

  • Central supplier database replacing distributed spreadsheets
  • Automated purchase order workflows with approval routing
PH 02

Payments and performance

  • Payment tracking with automated reminders against due dates
  • Supplier performance scoring to inform procurement decisions
  • Reporting dashboards for management oversight

The impact

03 · OUTCOME

Streamlined vendor operations

  • Missed payments and duplicate orders eliminated through single-system tracking
  • Supplier performance made visible and comparable
