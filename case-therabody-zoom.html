CASE STUDY &middot; ADVANCE ENGINEERING

Fourteen meeting rooms, two weeks, one deadline.

A full audio-visual overhaul of Therabody's Zoom Rooms, timed to land the day remote employees returned to the office.

The opportunity

01 · CONTEXT

Therabody's existing Zoom Rooms deployment was failing under everyday use. Virtual meetings and classes suffered frequent disruption: distorted audio, echo, participants who could not be understood, and video that froze or pixelated during important presentations.

The cost was more than inconvenience. Productivity dropped, and a company built on a reputation for polished, professional experiences was undermined every time an external client joined a broken call.

The window was two weeks. Employees were returning from remote work to onsite offices, and the rooms had to be right on day one.

The solution

02 · DELIVERY

Room-by-room checklists, developed and tested by a team of three, covering all fourteen spaces: Ajax, Amphitheater, Andes, Blanc, Denali, Rainer, El Capitan, Everest, Fuji, Half Dome, Machu Picchu, Matterhorn, Olympus, and T-HQ Global.

PH 01

Room-wise assessment and planning

  • Individual checklist built for each of the fourteen named rooms
  • Tasks, timelines, and resource allocation mapped per space
  • Unique acoustic and layout requirements documented room by room
PH 02

Equipment installation and configuration

  • Zoom Room equipment installed and configured in designated areas
  • Seamless functionality and user-friendly operation verified per room
PH 03

Software updates

  • Zoom software updated on iPad and Mac Mini for latest features and security patches
  • Apple software updates applied to guarantee compatibility and performance
PH 04

Audio and visual optimisation

  • Speaker and microphone volumes set for optimal audio quality in each space
  • Reverb resolved by tuning noise ratio settings within Zoom
  • TV display settings corrected to the proper aspect ratio for accurate content
PH 05

Compliance and handover

  • All work verified against industry standards and local codes
  • Compliance documentation prepared and delivered
  • Regular progress checkpoints maintained across the two-week window

The impact

03 · OUTCOME

Enhanced audio-visual performance

  • Average AV rating rose from 3.5 to 4.8 out of 5
  • Distortion, echo, and pixelation issues resolved across all rooms

User satisfaction

  • Satisfaction climbed from 75% to 95% post-upgrade

Technical issue resolution

  • Reported technical issues fell from an average of 2 per day to 0.5 per day

Compliance and engagement

  • Compliance adherence improved from 80% to 98%
  • Remote meeting participation increased by 30%
