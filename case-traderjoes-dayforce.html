520 stores, 21 days, zero downtime.

The opportunity

01 · CONTEXT

The brief was to upgrade Dayforce timeclock devices across more than 520 stores in under 33 days, immediately before the summer season. The stores spanned multiple states and time zones, and none of them could afford downtime.

Beyond the logistics, several existing timeclocks carried device-level faults that needed identifying and resolving in the field, with troubleshooting ranging from basic resets to advanced diagnostics. Where a device could not be recovered, it had to be swapped on the spot.

The objective was uninterrupted attendance management throughout, with no disruption to in-store staff during trading hours.

The solution

02 · DELIVERY

A team of four engineers plus two escalation support engineers, coordinated with an Eisenhower-matrix prioritisation model and scheduling driven by real store footfall data.

PH 01

Prioritisation and sequencing

  • Eisenhower matrix applied to categorise every store by urgency and importance
  • Stores grouped by time zone and geography to optimise engineer routing
  • Resource allocation mapped against the matrix to avoid idle capacity
PH 02

Footfall analysis

  • Daily people-flow data analysed for each store to identify customer traffic patterns
  • Upgrade windows scheduled against periods of lowest customer activity
  • Store-specific operational characteristics factored into each plan
PH 03

Three-tier communication protocol

  • Morning email at store opening with essential information and updates
  • Second email immediately before the software update commenced, preparing staff
  • Final email on successful completion confirming the transition to all store personnel
PH 04

Device remediation

  • Basic through advanced troubleshooting applied to faulty timeclock devices
  • Defective units replaced promptly where recovery was not viable
  • Escalation path staffed by two dedicated support engineers

The impact

03 · OUTCOME

Delivered ahead of schedule

  • Completed in 21 days against a 33-day deadline
  • Meticulous planning and time-zone sequencing drove the acceleration

Downtime avoided entirely

  • No disruption to in-store workers during trading hours
  • Downtime-related costs eliminated, yielding $20,000 in savings

Improved workforce management

  • Upgraded attendance system delivered more accurate employee time tracking
  • Operational efficiency raised across the full store network

Future-ready platform

  • Estate positioned on a technologically current, supportable system
  • Aligned to evolving business needs in a competitive retail sector
