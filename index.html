ORIGIN · WHY WE EXIST

A clinic in Islamabad. Midnight. A technician writing a graft count on a scrap of paper, because the system holding world-class surgery together lived on sticky notes, WhatsApp pings, and memory.

That image never left us. We didn't set out to build software. We set out to make that scrap of paper obsolete. Today we operate across healthcare, education, retail, and emerging technology, finding the inefficiencies others have accepted as normal, and engineering them away.
